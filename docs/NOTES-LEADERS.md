# Notes and leaders (4.2, front N2)

Writes are operations of `horizun_planimetry_ops` (rehearse with `dry_run`, apply with the token, re-read after commit, whole edit rolled back on any disagreement). Reads are modes of `horizun_query_planimetry`. Distances called `_mm` are paper millimetres unless the name says model.

## Writes
- `move_note`: `note_ids`, `delta_mm` [dx, dy], `frame` view|sheet (sheet goes through the viewport rotation), `keep_leader_ends` (default true: every end read back within 0.1 mm), `elbow` keep|horizontal|L, `side` keep|auto|left|right (left-aligned notes), `width_mm`, `text`, `fit_crop_margin_mm`. Covers move, flip, side, width, fit and shift of notes.
- `note_from_model`: `view_id`, `notes` [{`element_id`, `link_instance_id`?, `end_mm`, `note_mm`}] (model mm), `source` layers|material|type_name. The leader end must point at the element along the view direction (ray) or the call is refused. The element UniqueId and a signature of the text are stored on the note (extensible storage, schema HorizunNoteOriginV1).
- `note_from_model` also accepts `source=schedule` and `schedule_id`: the entire readable body becomes the note text, and the schedule identity and hash are stored on the note. Bodies over 1000 rows, 100 columns or 30000 characters, empty bodies and failed reads are refused. `required_notes` does not update these notes. `annotation_staleness` compares the recorded body with the current one; other tables and unassociated notes remain outside that coverage.
- `clone_tag`: `model_tag_id`, `targets` [{`element_id`, `head_mm`}], `sheet_horizontal`. One regeneration for the batch; target, head and orientation read back; a head at the model origin is refused; empty tag text is reported as evidence.
- `horizun_annotate` tag on a Room now accepts `add_leader`: the tag is born inside the room, the leader is switched on, then the head moves outside.

## Reads
- `leader_targets` (`view_ids` or `note_ids`): for each leader, measures its target along the view direction in the host model or a link. The result includes category, type, compound-structure layer, last changed by, and distance. The ray needs an existing 3D view. When it is unavailable, `coverage.ray = no_3d_view`. Unresolved nested links and unreadable cut planes give partial coverage. In a plan with a complete ray and no potentially visible loaded link whose bounds could contain the origin, the reader checks the host solids at the cut plane regardless of the first face's distance. A unique solid containing the origin takes precedence. A potentially overlapping link gives partial coverage and names the link instance. When a face was observed but the target is uncertain, `first_ray_hit` records the face separately from the target.
- `annotation_staleness` (`view_ids`, `changed_unique_ids`, `deleted_unique_ids`) checks every leader and counts each note once. A proven stale origin or target makes the note stale. Otherwise, any unconfirmed leader target leaves the note unknown, even when its origin matches. Findings include `leader_targets` with each leader's index, reading, verdict and reasons. The existing `leader_target` field still describes the first leader. Incomplete rays are reflected in `coverage.ray`, and `clean` requires complete ray and scoped schedule coverage and zero stale or unknown notes.
- `sheet_conflicts` (`sheet_id`, `gap_mm`, `include_linked_symbols`): box-box and line-box overlaps in sheet coordinates; linked symbols are approximated by the instance bounding box.
- `section_mark_diagnosis` (`view_id`, optional `section_view_id`; without it, every section view of the document up to `max_rows`): is the mark of the section in the view (viewer elements matched by name) and, when not, each cause measured: view range against the Z extent of the section crop box, Sections/Viewers hidden (view or template), hide-at-coarser-scale, view filters, phase filter, outside the view crop. A fact that cannot be read is `unknown` with its reason. Read only; `MoveElement` does not move the plane of a ViewSection.
- `free_spot` (`view_id`, `size_mm`, `near_mm` in sheet mm, `gap_mm`, `step_mm`, `max_radius_mm`): the nearest free place inside the viewport frame, computed without regenerating.

## Not covered (declared)
Dimension `move_by` and linked-family references in rotated viewports, tags with leaders in `leader_targets`, and the link between a finishes table and its notes.

## Round two
Writes (same `horizun_planimetry_ops`, same ensayo/token/relectura): `arrange_column` (`view_id`, `side` left|right, `width_mm`, `gap_mm`, optional `note_ids`), `set_leader_end` (one note, `leader_index`, `end_mm` or `delta_mm`, `frame`, `elbow`), `reseat_leaders` (`view_id` or `note_ids`, `max_shift_mm`; brings the end back to the nearest point of the bounding box of the element the note was made from), `required_notes` (as `note_from_model` but creates only the missing ones and reports present / stale), `replace_text` (`note_ids`, `old`, `new`, formatting kept), `sync_sheet_references` (`view_id`, `pattern` with one group, `map` {old sheet number: new}; without `map` it only reports references to sheets that do not exist).
Read: `view_ray` in `horizun_query_planimetry` (`view_id`, `points_mm`): first element met from each model point along the view direction, host or link.
`horizun_edit_dimensions`: new `move_by_sheet` [dx, dy] paper mm in the sheet frame, turned through the viewport rotation.

## Limits (declared)
- Empty text of a user-keynote tag: the keynote lives on the element TYPE; setting it is a model change (`horizun_set_keynote`), so `clone_tag` reports the cause (`target_keynote_value`) and does not write it.
- References to the center of a linked family in dimensions: not changed; the dimension API refuses datum references lifted through a link (see docs/LINKED-DIMENSIONS.md).

## Text notes in `horizun_annotate` (5.2)
New fields on an `operation=text` action (refused by name on any other operation):
- `width_mm`: the note width in **paper** mm (`TextElement.Width` is paper feet). Checked against `TextElement.GetMinimumAllowedWidth` / `GetMaximumAllowedWidth` of the text type; a value that only fits once divided by the view scale is refused saying it looks like a model length. It is also checked against the VIEW: wider than the paper width of the view's active crop (crop width over the scale; the crop height for a note turned to about 90 degrees), or, with no active crop, wider than 841 mm (on a sheet, wider than its widest title block, else 1219 mm), is refused in the rehearsal with the number it would be if it was a model length (`width_mm / scale`). `allow_outside_crop: true` places it that wide on purpose (it needs `width_mm`; `false` is ignored without it; a non-boolean is refused as such).
- `expected_lines`: the printed line count. Measured twice after creation: from `TextElement.Height` against a one-line and a two-line probe of the same text type (created in the rehearsal, rolled back), and from the view box. Both must agree; a count that cannot be told is `lines_uncertain` and fails closed. A mismatch rolls the whole call back. More lines than hard line breaks needs `width_mm`. The view box is axis-aligned, so a rotated note or a note in a 3D view is counted by its height alone (`box_count: skipped_rotated_or_3d`); with a leader the box is measured before the leader is added (`box_measured_before_leader`). The probes are created only when `expected_lines` is given.
- `leader_target`: `{point}` (model mm, projected on the note plane), `{element_id}` or `{link_instance_id, linked_element_id}` (nested links resolved). An element target can also include `point`. Without a point the end is aimed at the element by ray from a non-perspective 3D view (the target view itself when it is one). In an isometric the ray starts back toward the viewer or at the section box. A resolved `hit` names the document, link depth, category, id and type. An element target requires complete coverage and matching identity at rehearsal and after commit. A point by itself lets the bridge verify the stored arrow position when target coverage is partial. The hit evidence keeps that coverage and reports `proven: false`. A complete ray that meets nothing still refuses the point at rehearsal. Sheets, schedules, drafting views and legends accept only the point form.
- A complete ray that meets nothing also fails verification when cast from the stored end after commit, even within the end position tolerance.
- When post-commit verification fails, both apply modes attempt to roll back the whole group. The response confirms rollback only when Revit reports `RolledBack`. In `best_effort`, rows that passed the re-read then report `outcome: rolled_back`, `verified: false` and `in_model: false`. If Revit cannot confirm the rollback, their outcome is `uncertain` and `in_model` is null. The row that failed retains its measured evidence.
- `leader_side` auto|left|right and `leader_attachment` top|middle|bottom. A side that would put the elbow inside the text box is refused naming the other side. Verified after commit: leader count, end within 0.1 mm, anchor on the requested half, elbow outside the box, attachment.
- `visibility_warning` when the view hides annotation categories (`AreAnnotationCategoriesHidden`, typical of isometrics) or the Text Notes category, by the view or its template: the note is created but would not print.

Bounding-box trap: `Element.get_BoundingBox(view)` on an annotation has come back multiplied by the view scale (Revit 2024, field). Boxes of notes and tags are now checked against the annotation's own anchor (`TextNote.Coord`, tag head) and used as read, divided by the scale, or declared unreliable (`AnnotationBoxReader`, rule `TextNoteLayoutRules.NormalizeAnnotationBox`); `sheet_conflicts` and the planimetry box reads use it. `horizun_api_help` lists these traps under `field_traps` for TextNote / TextElement / Element.

`move_note` and `note_from_model` also check `width_mm` against the type's allowed range before writing.

Keynotes: covered without a new path. `horizun_set_keynote` sets the code on the type with its blast radius; `horizun_annotate` `tag` with a keynote tag type places the tag (`require_tag_text` refuses empty text).

Not covered: placing a leader end given in sheet coordinates through a viewport transform.
