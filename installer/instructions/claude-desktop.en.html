Connect Horizun Revit MCP to Claude Desktop

Three minutes. Every other client — Claude Code, Codex and ChatGPT Work — was already configured for you.

The file you need is in this same folder:
horizun-revit-__VERSION__.mcpb
  1. Open Claude Desktop and go to Settings. Click your name at the bottom left and choose Settings, or press Ctrl + ,.
  2. In the left column, under Desktop app, open Extensions. The first time, it will say No extensions installed.
  3. Drag the .mcpb file from this folder onto that window. The page says so itself: Drag .MCPB or .DXT files here to install. It is the shortest way and nothing has to be hunted for.
    Would rather not drag? Click Advanced settings, then Install extension. A file picker opens on Downloads: in its left column go to Documents, then Horizun-Revit-MCP, and choose the .mcpb. That button sits under a red developer-tools warning; that is normal and does not mean anything is wrong.
  4. Claude will warn you the extension gets access to your computer. Accept it and leave it Enabled. That warning appears for any MCP extension: it is the permission the bridge needs to talk to the Revit you have open. Everything happens on your machine; no server is involved.
  5. Close Claude Desktop and open it again. The tools do not appear until the app restarts.
  6. Open Revit with a model and ask Claude: “use horizun_health”. If it answers with the Revit version and the open document, it is working.
